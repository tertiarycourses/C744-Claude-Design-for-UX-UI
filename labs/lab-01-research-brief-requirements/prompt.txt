Goal: research-backed product brief, required-function list and measurable success criteria

Context: A neighbourhood wellness service needs a mobile experience for daily support and appointment booking.
Required content: Use realistic service names, appointment times, success, empty, loading, permission and error states.
Design system: Use the supplied tokens and component rules. Prefer clear labels, visible focus, 44px minimum targets and strong contrast.
Constraints: Mobile-first; no lorem ipsum; one dominant primary action per screen; preserve user input on recoverable errors.
Acceptance: Names a primary user and job; Separates evidence from assumptions; Lists essential functions; Defines observable success
Output: Create the named design in Claude Design, save a version checkpoint and export or share the evidence requested in the README.
